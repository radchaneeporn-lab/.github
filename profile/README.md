## About This Space

This place is managed by a skeptical girl who wants to understand how things work under the hood. She believes that learning at a fundamental level creates comprehensive, insightful understanding—enough to tackle both unexpected development challenges and real-world problems.

## Learning Philosophy

She shares content by linking concepts to their fundamentals while zooming out to show the big picture. When diving deep, it's easy to lose sight of the *why*, *what*, and *how* it all connects. 

Maintaining this big picture helps:
- Prevent information overload in a world of tremendous content
- Build real understanding that tackles real-world problems
- Keep the learning journey joyful by providing the topic for further learning


**To those who stop by this place:**  
I hope you find something valuable here—at least the energy to continue learning in a world full of wisdom for us to explore.
